What it does

Role-based secure access

Admin, student and visitor roles with JWT-protected login.

Announcements & live classes

Schedule sessions, share the Meet link, start and end classes.

Smart attendance

Auto join/leave tracking, present at attendance time, manual edit & publish.

Recorded lectures + chapters

YouTube recordings with clickable chapter timestamps, Coursera-style.

Teacher management

Maintain a teacher list with safe removal and reactivation.

Course-organized

Every class grouped under its course for clean, lasting records.

Discussion chat

Real-time messaging between teacher and students.

Notifications

Instant alerts for new classes, lectures and updates.

Secure uploads & moderation

Student uploads go live only after the teacher approves — with content moderation to keep the space safe.
